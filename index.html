Impreza WRX STI · 2003–2005 · Sheet 1 of 5

Flat four.

The engine under a Blobeye's bonnet scoop lies on its side. Four pistons, two each way, punching outward like a boxer's gloves.

Scroll to turn the drawing · Start the engine below

Sheet 2 · Plan viewLayout

Lying down

Seen from above, the cylinders point left and right instead of up. Cylinders 1 and 3 sit on the car's right, 2 and 4 on its left, with 1 at the front.

Each piston has a partner moving the opposite way at the same instant, so their shaking largely cancels. The engine is also flat and wide, which keeps its weight low in the car.

Sheet 3 · Front elevationFiring order

1 – 3 – 2 – 4

Every cylinder fires once in two turns of the crankshaft, so one fires every half turn. The order goes right bank twice, then left bank twice.

Sheet 4 · UndersideExhaust headers

The rumble

The engine fires evenly. The rumble comes from the pipes. With unequal-length headers, one bank's exhaust travels further to the collector, so its pulses arrive late and the beat goes lopsided.

Gaps between pulses: 180° · 270° · 180° · 90°

Switch to Equal in the dock and the pulses space out evenly. The note smooths out and the burble goes.

Pipe routing and delays are simplified to make the effect easy to see and hear.

Sheet 5 · Three-quarter viewRun it

Hold to rev

Start the engine, then press and hold Rev. Flip the headers while it runs and listen to the beat change.

The sound is generated live in your browser from the same firing model as the drawing. Nothing is recorded.
